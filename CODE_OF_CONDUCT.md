# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be
constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and respect
privacy and professional boundaries.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information.
Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `chrisdimurro@gmail.com`. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical and reviewed fairly.
